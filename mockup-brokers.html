The problem

Most deal presentations don't do justice to the business.

A PDF sent cold. A Dropbox link with unstructured financials. A listing on a classifieds site with three lines of description. Buyers scroll past. Conversations never start.

You've done the work to represent the seller. Ownly makes sure buyers can see it.

What you get

A deal page your clients will be proud of.

Structured deal pages
Every listing gets a clean, professional page — sector, location, headline metrics, description, highlights. Buyers get enough to self-qualify before requesting more.
NDA-gated financials
Full financial detail stays protected. Buyers who want to go further submit an enquiry — you're notified and in control of what gets shared.
Buyer enquiry routing
Enquiries from interested buyers come through Ownly and route directly to you. No cold calls to your client. No broker bypassing.
Shareable deal link
Each deal gets a clean URL you can share in your own outreach. Works as your deal summary, not a replacement for your process.

Important
