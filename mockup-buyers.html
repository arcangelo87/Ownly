The problem

Buying a small business takes longer than it should.

Finding a quality deal is hard enough. When you do find one, you spend weeks chasing the seller for documentation. Then more weeks finding the right lawyer, the right accountant — professionals who actually know SME transactions in this market.

And sometimes, after months of effort and real money spent, the business simply wasn't worth buying.

That's the reality most buyers face in Southern Europe. Ownly is built to change it.

"Three months and nothing to show for it."
